Privacy

{{ site.name }} has no backend. Your documents are read, processed and written entirely inside this browser tab — there is no upload endpoint they could be sent to.

Files are never transmitted

Choosing a file gives this page a reference to it in memory. Every tool works on that in-memory copy. Your file is never POSTed, streamed or beaconed anywhere.

No analytics on your documents

We do not record file names, types, sizes or contents. We could not attribute a conversion to you even if we wanted to, because the conversion happens on your machine.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for and no email address to hand over. What this site itself stores on your device is listed below.

Works offline

Once loaded, the service worker keeps the whole toolkit available without a connection — which is only possible because nothing needs a server round-trip.

Advertising

This site is free and is paid for by advertising. Ads are supplied by Google AdSense, a third party, which sets its own cookies on your device to measure and personalise what you see. Those cookies are not set by us and are not part of the list below.

For that, Google may use your IP address and the address of the page you are on. It never receives your documents. Everything above still holds: files are opened and converted on this device and are never sent anywhere, which is a property of how the tools work rather than a promise about who we sell to.

You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any personalised ad is loaded.

The fine print

Hosting

The site is served as static files. Whoever hosts those files can see standard web-server request logs — the pages and assets your browser fetched, your IP address and user agent. That is unavoidable for any website. It does not include anything about your documents, because your documents are never part of a request.

Third parties

Advertising is served by Google, which loads its scripts from Google's domains and sets its own cookies. That is the one third party involved, and what it does is described under Advertising above.

Nothing else is external. Every library the app uses is bundled and served from this domain, there are no embedded trackers or analytics scripts of our own, and no fonts are fetched from anywhere else.

Sharing features

Buttons like "Share" use your operating system's native share sheet, which hands the file to an app you pick. That is the one case where a file can leave this browser, and only because you explicitly chose a destination.

Children and sensitive data

We hold nothing about you. There is no account, no profile and no server-side record, so there is nothing for us to retain or to delete on request. Clearing your browser storage removes everything this site itself has ever kept.

Google's advertising cookies are separate from that and are governed by Google's own policies, which include its own controls for reviewing and deleting the data it holds.

Changes

If the architecture ever changes — for example if a tool genuinely required a server — that tool would be labelled clearly before you used it, and this page would say so.
